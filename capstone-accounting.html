About Us

Built on Trust, Driven by Results

Capstone Accounting & Tax was founded by Lance Brant, CPA, with a simple belief: clients deserve accounting partners who are proactive, accessible, and genuinely invested in their success.

Today, our team of experienced CPAs and accounting professionals serves clients across Oregon, Washington, Idaho, Colorado, Montana, and New Mexico, offering personalized service with the depth of a regional firm. With over 250 years of collective experience, we bring expertise that goes well beyond basic compliance.

Our Bend, Oregon headquarters is the heart of a growing practice built on lasting relationships, not transactional service.

Common Questions

Answers Before You Ask

Everything you need to know before booking your strategy call.

What's the difference between a bookkeeper and a CPA?
A bookkeeper records your transactions. A CPA like Shirley goes far deeper — analyzing your financial health, advising on tax strategy, planning for growth, and representing you before the IRS. TAS Firm does both, which means nothing slips through the cracks.
What size business do you typically work with?
We primarily serve businesses generating between $5,000 and $500,000 per month in revenue. Whether you're a growing startup or an established multi-six-figure operation, we have a service tier designed to match your stage and goals.
How quickly can you onboard us?
Most clients are fully onboarded within 5–7 business days. We have a streamlined process to get your books set up, your accounts connected, and your financial picture clear — fast.
What's the difference between bookkeeping and CFO services?
Bookkeeping keeps your records accurate. CFO services use those records to drive decisions — cash flow forecasting, growth modeling, scenario planning, and financial leadership. Many clients start with bookkeeping and add fractional CFO services as they scale.
Do you serve clients outside of Florida?
Yes — we serve clients across the U.S. and internationally for businesses with U.S. operations. We're also trilingual (English, Portuguese, Spanish), which allows us to serve a diverse, global client base with ease.
What if I'm facing an IRS audit or notice?
As an IRS Certified Acceptance Agent, Shirley can represent you directly before the IRS. Don't navigate audits or notices alone — reach out immediately and we'll assess your situation and build a defense strategy.

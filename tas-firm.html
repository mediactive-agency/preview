FAQ

Questions we

Still on the fence? These are the most common things business owners ask before their first call.

I already have a CPA. Do I still need a bookkeeper? +

Yes — and your CPA will thank you. CPAs file taxes. We keep your books clean and current all year so that when tax time comes, there are no surprises and no missed deductions. Clean books also mean lower CPA bills.

What’s the difference between Bookkeeping and CFO as a Service? +

Bookkeeping keeps your financial records accurate and up to date. CFO as a Service goes further — we analyze those numbers, identify where you’re leaking cash, and build a forward-looking strategy for growth. Most clients start with bookkeeping and add CFO when they’re ready to scale.

How long does it take to get started? +

Most clients are fully onboarded within 5–7 business days. If your books are significantly behind, we’ll give you a clear timeline upfront — no surprises.

What size business is this for? +

We work best with business owners doing $5K–$500K/month in revenue who are serious about growing. Not sure if you’re a fit? The diagnostic call will answer that for both of us.

Do I need to be based in Florida? +

No. We serve clients across the U.S. and internationally for businesses with U.S. operations. Everything runs remotely — nothing changes in how we work together.

What We Do

Talent Solutions for Every Hiring Need

Whether you need a single critical hire or an ongoing talent pipeline, we deliver solutions built around your organization's unique goals.

Direct Hire

Permanent placements sourced, screened, and delivered — with candidates who align to your culture and are ready to contribute from day one.

Contract Staffing

Flexible contract workers deployed quickly to fill short-term needs, support project spikes, or cover transitions without long-term commitment.

Executive Search

Senior and C-level searches conducted with discretion and precision — connecting you with leaders who drive culture and performance.

RPO Solutions

Recruitment Process Outsourcing for companies that need an embedded talent function — scalable, predictable, and aligned to your hiring goals.

Contract to Hire

Reduce risk by evaluating talent in role before committing. See performance, culture fit, and capability before making the permanent offer.

Talent Advisory

Strategic guidance on market conditions, compensation benchmarking, and workforce planning to help you hire smarter and faster.

Talent Acquisition Source was built on the belief that great recruiting is about understanding — your business, your culture, and what success actually looks like in the roles you need to fill. We do not treat every search the same, because every company and every role is different.

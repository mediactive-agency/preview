Our Story

Built on a Single Belief

Waya was founded on one conviction: single mothers represent a largely untapped talent group defined by grit, accountability, and an unmatched work ethic. Founder Malorie built Waya to change that narrative one placement at a time.

We partner with employers who recognize what resilience looks like in the workplace, and we work alongside candidates to ensure they are fully prepared and positioned to succeed.

Waya placements have delivered documented results: reduced turnover, stronger team culture, and employees who treat your business like their own.

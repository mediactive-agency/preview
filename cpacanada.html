Your Path to CPA

How to Earn Your CPA Designation

A structured, achievable pathway that can be completed while you work full-time.

1

Meet Prerequisites

Complete your undergraduate degree with the required subject area coverage to qualify for CPA PEP admission.

2

Complete CPA PEP

The CPA Professional Education Program takes 2 to 3 years, completed part-time while gaining practical experience.

3

Gain Experience

Complete 30 months of approved practical experience to develop the competencies required of a CPA.

4

Pass the CFE

Write the Common Final Examination near the end of CPA PEP and earn your designation.

Start Your CPA Journey

Led by Canada's Accounting Elite

CPA Canada was established in 2013 under the Canada Not-For-Profit Corporation Act, unifying the Canadian Institute of Chartered Accountants (CICA), CMA Canada, and CGA-Canada into a single, powerful national body.

Under the leadership of President and CEO Pamela Steer, FCPA, FCA, CFA, and newly appointed Chair of the Global Accounting Alliance, CPA Canada champions accounting best practices, represents the profession nationally and internationally, and prepares members for a rapidly evolving business environment.

Today, CPA Canada represents over 220,000 members and ranks among the largest national accounting bodies in the world, advocating in the public interest and upholding the highest standards of ethics and transparency.

About

A Digital-First CPA Firm Built for Modern Businesses

Counting Cloud was founded by Simran Kaur, CPA, to solve a problem she kept seeing: businesses growing fast but held back by outdated, reactive accounting. She built Counting Cloud to be everything a traditional firm is not — fast, digital, transparent, and proactive.

Based in Brampton, Ontario, we serve clients across the Toronto metro area and beyond through our fully cloud-based practice. Whether you're a law firm needing trust account management or a growing company ready to scale, we bring CPA-level expertise to every engagement.

Our promise: personal service with the speed and efficiency of a digital team. You'll always know where your money stands.

About Stacy

A mentor who speaks your language

Stacy Luft founded CEO Business Balance after recognizing a gap: service-based solopreneurs had plenty of accountants but very few financial mentors who could translate the numbers into plain, shame-free guidance.

Her approach is grounded in The Sovereign Three™ — her proprietary framework for helping practitioners understand cash flow, profitability, and sustainability without feeling overwhelmed. Stacy's goal isn't to make you into an accountant. It's to help you read your financial story and lead your business with confidence.

Meet Your Partner

With 25 years in accounting, Melanie built Willis Bookkeeping Services to give small business owners the kind of expert financial partnership previously only available to large corporations.

Her career began at a Northern Michigan District Court and grew through 23 years at a West Michigan accounting firm. Today she combines that deep institutional knowledge with the personal attention every small business deserves.

Melanie is an IRS Enrolled Agent, a triply certified QuickBooks ProAdvisor, an NTPI Fellow, and a Xero Certified Advisor. She serves Michigan businesses virtually, which means expert support without geographic limits.

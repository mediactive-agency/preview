About

Simple. Honest. That Is Our Whole Promise.

Coppersmith Recruitment and Services was built on a simple idea: that recruitment should be straightforward, transparent, and genuinely helpful — for both employers and job seekers.

Founded by two university students with a vision for better recruitment, we have grown into an award-winning agency serving businesses across the United Kingdom. GLAA certified and proudly independent.

Based in London, we serve clients nationwide across seven core sectors with the same commitment to honesty that has defined us from day one.

How It Works

From Search to Signed Offer — Fast

Job Search SAV streamlines the entire process so job seekers spend less time searching and more time interviewing — and employers spend less time screening and more time hiring.

1

Create Your Profile

Tell us your skills, experience, salary expectations, and what you are looking for in your next role — no generic resume uploads.

2

Get Matched

Our matching engine surfaces roles that fit your specific profile — and connects employers with candidates who are actually right, not just technically qualified on paper.

3

Interview Fast

No cold applications. No weeks of silence. Matches go straight to structured interviews with hiring managers who are ready to move.

4

Get the Offer

With both sides pre-qualified and aligned, offers move fast. Our average time from first match to signed offer is under two weeks.

For Employers

Stop Screening. Start Hiring.

Job Search SAV delivers pre-qualified, motivated candidates so your team spends time on interviews — not inbox triage.

Pre-Qualified Candidates

Every candidate you meet has been matched against your requirements and has opted in to your role — no cold outreach, no resume spam.

Fast Time-to-Hire

Average time from posting to first qualified match: under 48 hours. Our matching infrastructure eliminates the weeks of pipeline building.

Salary Transparency

All roles on Job Search SAV display salary ranges. That means candidates who apply are aligned on compensation before the first call.

ATS-Free Process

No complicated integrations. Post a role in minutes and get matched candidates delivered directly — without the ATS overhead.

Instant Shortlist

Get a curated shortlist of your top three to five matches within 48 hours — reviewed and ranked by our team before delivery.

Placement Guarantee

Every hire placed through Job Search SAV comes with a 60-day replacement guarantee. If it does not work, we find the replacement — fast.

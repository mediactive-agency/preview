What We Offer

Complete Financial Management for Your Business

From daily bookkeeping to fractional controller services, Prestige Bookkeeping handles the financial details so you can focus on running your business.

Bookkeeping Services

Daily record keeping, transaction categorization, bank reconciliation, and monthly financial reporting to keep your books accurate and current.

Payroll Processing

Reliable, timely payroll processing for your team. We handle calculations, withholdings, direct deposit, and payroll tax filings accurately every cycle.

Accounting Software Setup and Training

QuickBooks setup, configuration, and personalized training so your team can manage finances confidently and get the most from your software investment.

Fractional Controller Services

Senior-level financial oversight without the full-time cost. We provide budgeting, financial analysis, cash flow management, and reporting at the executive level.

Financial Catch-Up Projects

Behind on your books? We specialize in organizing and reconciling months or years of backlogged records so your financials are clean, current, and ready for tax season.

Sales Tax and Secretary of State Filings

Accurate, on-time sales tax returns and required Secretary of State annual filings to keep your business compliant and penalty-free throughout the year.

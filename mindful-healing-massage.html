Catrina Braatz Weed, LMT

Catrina Braatz Weed, LMT, is the founder of Mindful Healing Massage LLC and a licensed massage therapist with over 17 years of experience. A graduate of Lakeside School of Massage Therapy, Catrina holds credentials as an LMT, ADMT, and 200-hour Certified Yoga Teacher.

Her work spans therapeutic, sports, and deep tissue massage, neuromuscular release, hot stone, gua sha, foot reflexology, and meditation — creating a practice that treats the whole person, not just the symptom. Located in Suite 10 at N28W23000 Roundy Drive in Pewaukee, WI, Mindful Healing Massage is a space dedicated to everyone who is trying to heal and needs a helping hand.

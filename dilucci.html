Our Story

A Father-Daughter Firm with Deep Roots in Dallas

DiLucci CPA Firm was founded in 2005 on a simple idea: business owners deserve more than a tax preparer — they deserve a financial partner. Jasmine DiLucci, JD, CPA, EA, built the firm into a full-service powerhouse, joined by her father John DiLucci, EA, CTRS.

Together, they lead a 35+ person team of credentialed accountants, enrolled agents, and financial specialists — all focused on one thing: helping you keep more of what you earn.

About

Meet Matt Hamilton, CPA

Matt Hamilton, CPA, founded Surefire Tax and Accounting to solve a specific problem: most real estate investors get their books done by one firm and their tax advice from another, and those two advisors never talk to each other.

Surefire integrates bookkeeping and strategic advisory into a single engagement, so the advisor who closes your books also advises on your exits, deal structures, and partner allocations. Specializing in Subchapter K partnership taxation, cost segregation, 1031 exchanges, bonus depreciation, and the STR loophole, Matt brings niche depth to every engagement.

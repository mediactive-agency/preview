About Unifaction

Built on a Belief That Great Expertise Should Be Accessible

Jia Yi Duong, CPA, founded Unifaction on the belief that great financial expertise should be accessible without the cost and commitment of permanent headcount. With over 15 years in accounting and finance, Jia built a trusted network of senior CPAs and financial professionals who share her commitment to quality, flexibility, and results.

Unifaction's consultants have backgrounds at firms like Deloitte and across diverse sectors — bringing institutional-level knowledge to organizations of any size. The mission is simple: be the reliable partner your finance team needs, exactly when you need them.

Common Questions Answered

Not sure if CountDigital is right for your situation? We've answered the most common questions below.

Ask Us Directly →
Do I need to leave my current CPA?
No — CountDigital is explicitly designed as a "Keep-Your-CPA" service. We work alongside your existing tax professional, delivering reconciled data and IRS-ready reports that your CPA incorporates directly into your return. Your CPA relationship stays intact.
What if I have transactions on many different platforms?
That's exactly the complexity we specialize in. We reconcile across every major exchange (Coinbase, Kraken, Binance, etc.), self-custody wallets, and on-chain activity across multiple blockchains. The more complex your portfolio, the more value we provide.
Can you handle DeFi activity like staking and yield farming?
Yes. DeFi advanced accounting is one of our core services. We manually reconcile liquidity pool deposits and withdrawals, yield farming rewards, token wrapping, bridging events, and other complex DeFi interactions — applying correct tax treatment to each event type.
What deliverables will my CPA receive?
Your CPA receives a fully reconciled transaction history, capital gain and loss calculations, IRS Form 8949 summary, Schedule D data, and a written reconciliation report outlining our methodology and findings. Everything needed to prepare and file the return confidently.
Do you work with businesses as well as individuals?
Yes. We serve individual investors, high-net-worth clients, and businesses that accept cryptocurrency or maintain digital asset holdings. Business engagements include both tax reporting and financial statement support.
How do I get started?
The easiest first step is booking a free 20-minute consultation. We'll assess your portfolio complexity, answer your questions, and walk you through our process. There's no commitment — just clarity on your crypto tax situation.

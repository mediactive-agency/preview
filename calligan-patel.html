What We Do

Tax & Accounting Services for Every Stage

Whether you are an individual, a small business owner, or a corporation, we provide the expertise and personal attention you deserve.

Personal Tax

From employed individuals to rental property owners, retirees, and students, we file your return accurately and find every deduction you are entitled to.

Small Business Accounting

Annual financial statements, corporate tax returns, HST remittances, and day-to-day accounting support for growing businesses.

Corporate Tax Returns

Preparing and filing corporate T2 returns with careful attention to available deductions, deadlines, and CRA compliance.

HST Remittances

Accurate calculation and timely filing of HST returns, keeping you compliant and avoiding costly CRA penalties.

CRA Representation

Dealing with the CRA on your behalf, including audit support, correspondence management, and dispute resolution.

Business & Tax Advisory

Strategic guidance on structuring your business to optimize your tax position today and protect your wealth into the future.

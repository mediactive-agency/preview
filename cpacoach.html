Meet Your Coach

Rose Y.C. Huie, C.P.A.

Rose Y. C. Huie is an accounting practice coach and mentor with over 37 years of professional experience. She built and successfully sold a profitable accounting practice, giving her firsthand knowledge of what it truly takes to create a thriving CPA firm.

A graduate of UC Berkeley with both a B.S. in Accounting and Finance and an MBA in Accounting, Rose brings academic rigor and real-world experience to every coaching relationship. She is a Certified Public Accountant, a Certified Financial Planner, and a licensed insurance agent in California.

Today, Rose dedicates herself to helping CPAs and professionals increase their income and happiness through CPA coaching, Positive Intelligence coaching, and relationship coaching programs designed around each client's unique needs.

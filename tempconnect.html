About Temp Connect

Built to Solve the Hiring Chaos

Temp Connect was created to give employers and workers a better way to find each other — and stay connected. Based in Aurora, Colorado, our platform serves businesses and professionals across the country and around the world.

We built Temp Connect because hiring should not require five different tools and a dozen email threads. From your first posting to your first day on the job, everything lives in one streamlined platform — designed for the modern, flexible workforce.

About P3

Built to create long-term value through disciplined, hands-on ownership.

Founded in 2014 by Harvey Rosenblatt, P3 was built to create long-term value through disciplined, hands-on real estate ownership. Over the past decade, the firm has developed a track record of acquiring and repositioning assets across the office, multifamily, retail, and mixed-use sectors, with a focus on value-add opportunities.

We target fundamentally sound properties that are underperforming due to outdated strategy, operational inefficiencies, or misaligned ownership. From acquisition through stabilization, we execute with a clear plan, rigorous underwriting, and deliberate operational focus — because the gap between an asset's potential and its current performance is precisely where P3 creates value.

Our vertically integrated platform is what sets us apart. We manage acquisitions, asset management, property management, and leasing in-house — giving us complete control over execution and complete accountability for results. No outsourcing of responsibility. No misalignment of incentives.

Investment Process

How P3 Creates Value

A disciplined, repeatable process built around conservative underwriting, in-house execution, and patient capital deployment.

Step 01

Disciplined Acquisition

We underwrite conservatively and stress-test every assumption. We only buy what we believe in and would bet our own capital on. If it only works in the best case, we pass.

Step 02

Strategic Repositioning

Capital investment, operational improvement, and market repositioning that unlocks the gap between an asset's current performance and its potential. We find what others miss.

Step 03

In-House Operations

Our integrated management and leasing platforms mean we control every decision at the property level. No outsourced accountability. Every call is ours to make — and ours to own.

Step 04

Optimized Disposition

We choose timing and structure based entirely on maximizing risk-adjusted returns for our investors — not on fund timelines, fee structures, or external pressures.

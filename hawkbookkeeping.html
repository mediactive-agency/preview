What We Offer

Financial Clarity for Every Stage of Life

Whether you run a small business, manage a household, or need support for an aging loved one, Hawk Bookkeeping has a solution built around your needs.

Small Business Bookkeeping

Clean, dependable books for service-based businesses, trades, and contractors. Monthly reconciliation, accurate financial statements, and decision-ready reporting.

Household Bookkeeping

Organize your accounts, manage bill payments, and track cash flow. Bringing structure and clarity to the financial lives of busy professionals and families.

Senior Financial Support

About Adrian Hawk

A Systems Thinker Who Loves Clean Numbers

Before founding Hawk Bookkeeping, Adrian Hawk spent over a decade in federal service specializing in simulation modeling and business process improvement. He brings that same disciplined, systems-driven mindset to every client's financial records.

Based in Woodbine, Maryland, Adrian serves local clients and businesses virtually across the region. His approach is simple: deliver practical, professional support that gives clients genuine clarity and control over their finances.

Adrian is pursuing Profit First certification and was elected Treasurer of LeTip of Howard County Maryland, a testament to the trust his fellow business professionals place in his expertise and integrity.

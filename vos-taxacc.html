About the Firm

Financial Leadership Built for the Way You Do Business

VOS Accounting & Tax Services was founded in 2011 with a single mission: to give growing businesses access to the kind of financial leadership that was previously only available to large corporations.

We are a veteran-owned firm based in Cumming, Georgia, serving professional services, construction, healthcare, real estate, and international businesses from $500K to $10M+ in revenue across 6 countries.

Our proprietary VosCierge delivery model pairs each client with a dedicated financial advisor backed by automated processes, delivering clean books, sharp strategy, and real-time dashboards at a predictable fixed-fee retainer, with no hourly billing ever.

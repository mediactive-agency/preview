Our Philosophy

We Build Relationships, Not Pipelines

In a world of mass outreach and automated recruiting, Newcastle takes the opposite approach. Every search begins with listening — to the company, to the candidate, to what truly matters beyond the job description.

Marc Basil founded Newcastle on the belief that the best placements happen when the recruiter deeply understands both sides. That takes time, curiosity, and genuine care — all things that cannot be automated.

Meet the Founder

Marc Basil

With over 25 years in executive search and recruitment, Marc Basil has built Newcastle Search Group into one of the most trusted boutique search firms in finance and accounting placement.

Based in Chicago, Marc works with companies across the country who are tired of impersonal, transactional recruiting. His approach is simple: know the people on both sides of the placement better than anyone else in the room.

Marc conducts thorough candidate interviews, provides honest coaching, and maintains relationships long after placements are made — because in his world, reputation is everything.

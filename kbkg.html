What We Do

Comprehensive Tax Incentive Services

From cost segregation to R&D credits, we identify every dollar Congress has made available to your business.

Cost Segregation

Accelerate depreciation on your real estate to generate significant upfront tax savings. Any building improvement over $1.5M should be reviewed.

R&D Tax Credit

Federal credit worth approximately 6.5% of qualifying wages for companies designing, developing, and improving products, processes, or software.

Section 179D Deduction

Federal deduction worth up to $5.65 per square foot for energy efficient commercial and government buildings.

Section 45L Tax Credit

$2,000 credit per unit for energy efficient residential properties including apartments, condominiums, and single family developments.

Negotiated Incentives

State and local government incentive programs for business investment, facility expansion, and job creation. Location advisory included.

Repair vs. Capitalization Review

Identify expenses that should be treated as current deductions rather than capitalized, covering HVAC systems, roofs, and parking lots.

About Us

Built for Trade Service Businesses

Luis Serrano founded Serrano Bookkeeping to serve the trade service businesses that keep homes and businesses running — especially painting contractors who are too busy to keep up with their books.

With 5+ years of hands-on bookkeeping experience and deep expertise in QuickBooks Online, Luis brings family values and genuine care to every client relationship. Based in San Diego, his team operates on a simple principle: clean books and clear next steps. No jargon, no surprises, just reliable financial clarity so you can focus on your craft.

Services are offered at flat monthly rates — because uncertainty in pricing is the last thing a small business owner needs.

What We Do

Controller-Level Services Built for Growth

From monthly reconciliations to audit prep, we handle the financial complexity so you can focus on scaling.

Accrual-Based Bookkeeping
Monthly reconciliations across multiple payment systems, revenue recognition for subscription models, and project profitability tracking with clear P&L, balance sheet, and cash flow statements.
Controller-Level Oversight
Variance analysis, cost accounting, inventory tracking, accrual adjustments, and internal control oversight to ensure audit-ready books and investor-ready reporting at every stage.
Compliance and Risk Management
1099 issuance, contractor oversight, tax-ready financial packages, and audit-prep documentation for regulated industries and high-growth companies operating in complex environments.
Financial Reporting and KPI Tracking
Chart of accounts setup, KPI dashboard creation, and cash flow forecasting to give leadership the real-time visibility needed to make confident, data-backed decisions.
Historical Financial Cleanup
Comprehensive review and correction of historical records, preparing your books for audits, funding rounds, or new ownership with clean, reliable financial documentation.
Growth-Focused Advisory
Pricing and margin analysis, fundraising preparation for SaaS clients, and industry-specific advisory for agencies, cannabis retailers, and other growth-oriented businesses.

About Higher Level Accounting

Strategic Numbers. Confident Decisions.

Higher Level Accounting provides strategic, controller-level accounting services for growth-minded businesses. Our focus is on delivering accurate, accrual-based financials, actionable insights, and rock-solid compliance, so you can scale confidently and never wonder if your numbers are telling the truth.

Over the years, we have worked closely with founders, operators, and leadership teams across our three core verticals and beyond. In every case, the business leaders we meet are experts in their craft. However, without the right financial processes, even great companies risk fines, lost licenses, missed funding rounds, or costly missteps.

Founded by Alvonn Forsyth, MBA, Higher Level Accounting brings corporate accounting discipline to small and mid-size businesses. Alvonn's background spans corporate finance, pharmaceutical startups, cannabis retail, SaaS companies, and creative agencies, giving each client access to the kind of financial leadership typically reserved for enterprise teams.

Our Story

Led by Katie Warnock, Built for Mission-Driven Organizations

Staffing Boutique was founded by Katie Warnock with a clear purpose: to bring boutique-level attention and responsiveness to the organizations that need people most — schools, nonprofits, and healthcare providers.

As a certified Woman-Owned Business Enterprise (WBE), we bring both expertise and accountability to every engagement. With $10M+ in revenue generated and a roster of loyal NYC clients, we've proven that great staffing is about relationships, speed, and genuine care.

What We Do

End-to-End Machining Recruitment

We handle the entire recruiting process for machining and manufacturing roles — so your team can stay focused on production, not hiring.

CNC Machining Roles

CNC machinists, operators, programmers, and setup technicians — sourced from job boards, resume databases, and direct outreach to active and passive candidates.

Fabrication & Welding

Structural welders, MIG/TIG specialists, fabricators, and shop-floor production staff placed with fabrication shops and manufacturing facilities.

Precision Manufacturing

Quality technicians, inspection, and precision manufacturing specialists for aerospace, medical device, defense, and high-tolerance production environments.

Full-Cycle Sourcing

We handle everything: job postings, LinkedIn outreach, resume mining, phone screens, and candidate delivery — so you only spend time on the best fits.

Scalable RPO Model

Industry-Expert Team

About JPR

Built by Machining People, for Machining Companies

J. Peace Recruiting was founded by Justin with one conviction: the machining industry deserves a recruiting model built specifically for it. Not a generic staffing agency, not a contingency headhunter charging 20% per hire — a dedicated, flat-rate fractional RPO that knows the difference between a G-code programmer and a setup tech.

We have decades of real shop-floor, machining industry experience across CNC, fabrication, welding, and precision manufacturing. That expertise means we write better job posts, ask the right screening questions, and recognize a strong candidate before you ever have to interview them.

Our model is simple: you pay a flat monthly rate, we recruit as many qualified people as you need, and you hire without limits. Pause anytime. No contracts. No surprises.

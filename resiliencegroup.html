What We Do

Connecting Companies With Their Best People

Our three-pillar approach addresses every stage of the talent lifecycle — from finding and placing exceptional leaders to supporting professionals through career transitions.

Executive Search

We identify and place exceptional leaders who fit your culture and drive measurable results. Every search is custom-tailored — never a one-size-fits-all approach.

HR Consulting

Strategic people operations support for organizations navigating growth, change, or workforce challenges. Practical guidance grounded in real-world HR experience.

Outplacement Services

We support professionals through career transitions with coaching, resume strategy, and negotiation guidance — because every ending should lead to a better beginning.

Cultural Fit Assessment

We spend time understanding your organization's culture, values, and team dynamics before ever recommending a candidate — because fit matters as much as credentials.

Candidate Coaching

From positioning to offer negotiation, we prepare candidates to present their best selves and make strategic career moves that align with long-term goals.

Workforce Strategy

Long-term talent planning aligned with your business strategy — helping you build sustainable, high-performing teams for the next phase of growth.

About Us

Built on Integrity. Driven by Impact.

Resilience Group is a Strongsville, Ohio-based talent firm dedicated to connecting companies with the people who will shape their future. Led by Natalie Lemons, our team brings a people-first philosophy to every search — investing in relationships before results.

We don't believe in filling positions. We believe in making the right match: the candidate who fits not just on paper, but in culture, character, and long-term potential.

Whether you need to find a senior leader, navigate a workforce challenge, or support a professional through a career transition — we are here to make it a success.

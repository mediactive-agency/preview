Our Story

20 Years of Connecting People to Their Best Work

Orlando Williams founded Motus in 2006 after recruiting for Nike. He previously worked with University of Oregon athletics and the Harlem Globetrotters — experiences that shaped his belief that people are an organization's most valuable asset, and that finding the right fit changes everything.

Based in Tigard, Oregon, Motus has grown into a team of dedicated recruiters serving clients across the Pacific Northwest and nationwide. We prioritize inclusive hiring practices, genuine relationships, and results that hold up over time.

With a 4.6-star rating on ClearlyRated and 268+ clients served, our track record speaks for itself — but we are most proud of the careers and cultures we have helped build along the way.

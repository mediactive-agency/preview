What I Offer

Bookkeeping Services Built for Service Businesses

Every service is designed to give you clear, accurate financials and the freedom to focus on what you actually do best.

Monthly Bookkeeping

Transaction categorization, bank and credit card reconciliation, payroll processing, accounts receivable and payable, and 1099 filings.

Financial Reporting

Profit and loss statements, balance sheets, job costing, inventory tracking, and fixed asset management — all delivered clearly.

Catch-Up Bookkeeping

Behind on your books? I get you caught up quickly so you have clean, accurate records from day one going forward.

Full QuickBooks Online setup tailored to your service business so your books work the way your business actually works.

Payroll Processing

Accurate, on-time payroll processing so your team gets paid right and your payroll records stay clean and compliant.

Ongoing Advisory

Regular check-ins to review your numbers, answer questions, and help you understand what your financials are telling you.

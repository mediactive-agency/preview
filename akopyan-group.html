Our Services

Tax Planning for Business Owners

Proactive strategy to minimize your tax liability all year long, not just at filing time. Regular check-ins and strategic support included.

Bookkeeping and Accounting

Business and Personal Tax Services

Payroll Management

Meet Yuriy Akopyan, CPA

A Certified Public Accountant since 2002, Yuriy founded The Akopyan Group with a clear mission: to give Seattle-area small businesses the same quality financial guidance that large corporations enjoy, at a cost that makes sense.

As an entrepreneur himself, Yuriy understands the pressures, challenges, and ambitions of running a small business. He leads an experienced team of professionals who create and execute operational and tax strategies for businesses of all sizes.

Since 1999, The Akopyan Group has grown to serve hundreds of businesses across three Washington state locations, with a reputation built on responsiveness, integrity, and results. Yuriy is also active in charitable work within the Armenian community.

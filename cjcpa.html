About CJCPA

An Accountant With a Heart

Jas Chandi, CPA, CGA, is the founder of CJCPA and has built one of British Columbia's most trusted accounting firms over more than a decade of dedicated service. Known as "an accountant with a heart," Jas brings a personal, client-first approach to every engagement — whether it is a startup finding its footing or an established enterprise navigating complex transactions.

He is joined by Gary Traher, CPA, CA, and Shiv Chaudhary, CIA, MA, a team with deep expertise in Canadian and US taxation, financial auditing, and business advisory. Together, they serve clients across Langley, Abbotsford, Surrey, and beyond.

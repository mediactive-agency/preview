Meet Tammy

Bookkeeping Built on Trust and Care

Hi, I'm Tammy, the owner of Tammy's Bookkeeping Boutique. I'm based in Oshawa, Ontario, where I live with my husband and our two teenage children.

With over 7 years of experience in the bookkeeping and tax industry, I've had the opportunity to work alongside CPAs and gain hands-on knowledge that has shaped both my expertise and my confidence. That experience inspired me to create a boutique-style bookkeeping service built on trust, care, and attention to detail.

My goal is simple: to help small business owners and individuals feel confident, organized, and at ease when it comes to their finances. I specialize in QuickBooks Online bookkeeping and Canadian personal income taxes, offering clear, supportive, and tailored solutions that fit your unique needs.

I believe bookkeeping should feel approachable, transparent, and supportive. My role is to take the stress off your plate so you can focus on what you do best.

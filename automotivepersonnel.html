Our Story

Over 30 Years Driving the Automotive Industry Forward

Automotive Personnel, LLC was founded by Donald Jasensky in 1989 with one clear mission: to bring world-class recruiting expertise specifically to the automotive sector. For over three decades, we have been the go-to recruiting partner for dealership groups, finance companies, and aftermarket businesses nationwide.

Based in Tampa, Florida, we operate Mon-Fri 9am-5pm and Saturday mornings — because the automotive industry doesn't follow a standard 9-to-5 schedule, and neither do we.

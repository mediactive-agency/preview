About Spa Bleu

Spa Bleu was founded with a single commitment: create a peaceful atmosphere where every guest experiences genuine beauty and wellness care at the highest level. With three locations across the Chicago suburbs — West Dundee, South Barrington, and St. Charles — Spa Bleu has built a reputation for expert service, Aveda-quality products, and a team of dedicated professionals who truly love what they do.

As a Salon Today 200 Honoree and Green Circle Salon, Spa Bleu combines award-winning results with a commitment to sustainability. Whether you are coming in for a cut and color or a full spa day, the focus is always the same: 5-star service, every visit.

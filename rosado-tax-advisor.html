Our Services

Comprehensive U.S. tax advisory and compliance for individuals, companies, trusts, and estates both domestic and international.

U.S. Tax Advisory Services

Expert tax advice on investing in U.S. real estate, pre-immigration planning, U.S. estate tax planning, structuring businesses, and IRS controversy matters. Tangible solutions tailored to your unique situation.

U.S. Tax Compliance Services

Preparation of U.S. federal and state tax returns and international information returns for individuals, companies, trusts, and estates. IRS compliance assistance for those not current with U.S. tax obligations.

International Tax Planning

Strategic guidance for U.S. and non-U.S. clients on international tax compliance, FIRPTA planning, foreign trust reporting, and cross-border transactions involving the U.S. and Puerto Rico.

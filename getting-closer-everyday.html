About Jenna

One Transformation Became a Movement

Jenna Leveille started Getting Closer Everyday in 2015 after losing 140 pounds in just 18 months. What began as a personal transformation became a movement when she realized the one thing that made the difference was accountability and community.

Today, GCE has grown to over 130,000 members who show up for each other every day — sharing wins, supporting struggles, and proving that progress is possible. Jenna believes that when you learn something in life, it is only great when it is shared.

Her mission is to give every member the tools, community, and motivation to close the gap between where they are and where they want to be.

Services

Every Aspect of Condominium Management, Covered

From day-to-day administration to long-term capital planning, CIE handles every dimension of condo management so your board can focus on what matters most — your community.

Administrative Management

Complete administrative support including board meeting facilitation, record-keeping, owner communications, and compliance documentation.

Financial Management

Accurate budgeting, monthly financial reporting, reserve fund management, and transparent accounting that keeps your board fully informed.

Human Resources

Professional management of on-site staff, contractors, and service providers — from hiring through performance management and compliance.

Physical Maintenance

Responsive maintenance coordination for common elements, repairs, and building systems — with vendors you can trust and costs you can control.

Preventative Maintenance

Proactive maintenance programs that extend asset life, reduce emergency costs, and protect your corporation's long-term financial health.

Project Management

End-to-end oversight of capital improvement projects — from planning and tendering through execution and final inspection.

About CIE

Driven by Integrity. Powered by Efficiency.

CIE Property Management and Consulting was built on a simple but powerful idea: condo management should work for the people who live in and own the buildings being managed — not the other way around.

For nearly a decade, CIE has delivered integrity-driven management across Ontario, earning recognition as the 2019 Milton Chamber of Commerce Small Business of the Year. Our approach is what sets us apart: we combine professional expertise with genuine responsiveness, treating every issue — whether it is a maintenance request or a board dispute — with the care and urgency it deserves.

We are proud members of ACMO and the CCI, and we stay current on every legislative change affecting condo corporations in Ontario so your board never has to.

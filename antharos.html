Meet the Founder

Built on the Belief That Recruiting Can Change Lives

Ted Woolsey is the founder of Antharos Recruiting, a firm built on the belief that recruiting can do more than fill seats — it can change lives. With nearly a decade in the staffing and recruiting industry, Ted has placed hundreds of sales professionals across the Northeast and nationwide.

His background at Kforce gave him a front-row seat to what separates great hires from costly ones. Today, he channels that experience into building teams for companies that care about quality, culture, and community.

Every match Antharos makes contributes to a charitable cause — because Ted believes a great hire should benefit more than just the bottom line.
